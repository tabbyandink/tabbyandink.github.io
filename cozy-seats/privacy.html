Cozy Seats: Family Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 Developer: Tabby & Ink Contact: tabbyandink@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the game "Cozy Seats: Family Puzzle" (the "Game") collects, why, and what choices you have.

1. Short version

  • The Game has no accounts and no chat. We never ask for your name, e‑mail, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress (solved levels, family photos in your album, daily puzzles, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows short video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example "watch a video for an extra hint"). It may also show a rare full‑screen ad between levels after level 15 (never during play, never in the daily puzzle, never for players who bought "no ads").
  • The Game keeps a small local log of game events (level started/won/lost) on your device only, to help us balance levels when testers send it to us themselves.

2. Data stored on your device

Two small files: your progress and settings, and a local event log. They never leave your device unless you choose to send them to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

2a. Sharing photos

When you tap "Share", the Game opens your phone's own share menu with the family photo picture (it is a drawing made by the Game, not a camera photo). If sharing is not available, the picture is saved to your Pictures folder. Nothing is uploaded by us.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To show ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect: the advertising ID of your device, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about ad interactions.

  • In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown first, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal data from the ads; we only see aggregated reports.

4. Purchases

If the Game offers a one‑time purchase (for example "no ads"), payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for everyone, but it is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights and contact

Questions or requests: tabbyandink@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access or delete data and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
