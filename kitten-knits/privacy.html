Kitten Knits: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 Developer: Tabby & Ink Contact: tabbyandink@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the game "Kitten Knits: Cozy Yarn Sort" (the "Game") collects, why, and what choices you have.

1. Short version

  • The Game has no accounts and no chat. We never ask for your name, e‑mail, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress (levels, hearts, knitted items, the daily sock, settings and cosy‑corner choices) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows short video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example "watch a video for one more hint"). It may also show a rare full‑screen ad between levels after level 15 (never during play, never in the first session, never in the daily sock, never for players who bought something).
  • When you tap Share, the Game makes a postcard picture of the item you knitted and opens your phone's own share sheet, or saves the picture to your gallery. It goes only where you send it.

2. Data stored on your device

One small file with your progress and settings. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To show ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect: the advertising ID of your device, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about ad interactions.

  • In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown first, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal data from the ads; we only see aggregated reports.

4. Purchases

The Game offers optional one‑time purchases: "No ads" (no full‑screen ads, and the helpers an ad would give come free) and three thank‑you packs that only bring cosy decorations and never make a level easier. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The Game only stores on your device which products you own, so they come back if you reinstall the Game with the same Google account.

4a. Rating the Game

Now and then, after a happy win, the Game may open Google Play's own "rate this game" card. Google handles it; we are not told whether you rated or what you wrote, and nothing in the Game depends on it.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for everyone, but it is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights and contact

Questions or requests: tabbyandink@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access or delete data and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
